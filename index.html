Landmark Capital Advisors Private Limited

Landmark Capital Advisors Private Limited is a Mumbai-based real estate investment management firm focused on identifying, structuring and managing investment opportunities across India's evolving real estate sector. Established in 2013, the firm brings experience in institutional real estate investments, private equity and asset management, with a focus on creating long-term value through disciplined investment strategies.

About Landmark Capital Advisors

Through its understanding of market fundamentals, asset potential and changing investment requirements, Landmark Capital Advisors works across real estate segments, including warehousing, residential and commercial properties. Its approach combines financial expertise, operational involvement and a structured understanding of investment opportunities.

India's real estate investment landscape is undergoing significant changes, driven by urbanisation, infrastructure expansion, institutional capital and the growing demand for organised real estate assets. Landmark Capital Advisors focuses on opportunities emerging from these developments, with an emphasis on investment discipline, market research and asset-level performance.

With experience in real estate private equity and institutional fund management, the firm has participated in the development of investment opportunities across multiple property segments. Its investment approach considers market demand, location fundamentals, financial feasibility and operational requirements to evaluate the potential of different assets.

Our Investment Focus

Warehousing and Logistics

The expansion of e-commerce, manufacturing and organised supply chains has increased demand for modern warehousing infrastructure. The firm focuses on opportunities within this evolving asset class, where location, connectivity and operational efficiency play an important role.

Residential Real Estate

India's residential property market continues to evolve with changing homebuyer preferences, urban development and infrastructure investments. The firm explores opportunities supported by market demand and long-term growth potential.

Commercial Real Estate

Commercial properties remain an important part of India's institutional investment landscape. Asset quality, tenant demand, location advantages and income potential are key considerations in evaluating this segment.

Private Equity and Structured Investments

Real estate private equity involves identifying opportunities that require financial expertise, structured capital and active asset management. The firm's investment approach focuses on evaluating opportunities across different stages of the real estate lifecycle.
